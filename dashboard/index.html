How the model works

Every protocol on this board records no token: no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol in the snapshot. Its implied value is what the market would plausibly say it is worth if it issued one today, and the model derives that from the snapshot's own figures — never from a hand-picked list of names:

implied = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × breadth

  • Category multiple — calibrated, not chosen. For each category, the model takes every protocol in the same snapshot that does trade a token, computes its market cap over TVL, and uses the median, shrunk toward the global median by sample size. A category with three wild observations cannot shout down three hundred quiet ones — and a category with fewer than three comps of its own is not trusted at all: the global median speaks for it.
  • Momentum. The 7-day TVL change, passed through a tanh so one hot week bends the value by at most the momentum weight instead of breaking it.
  • Maturity. A saturating curve over the protocol's age: surviving longer counts, with diminishing returns. Unknown ages are scored at a neutral 270 days.
  • Breadth. A small premium per extra chain beyond the first, capped — distribution is real, but it is not magic.
  • Who is ranked. Every watchlist protocol (TVL at or above the snapshot's floor, recording no token), plus every other rankable pre-token protocol in the snapshot. Versioned pools, exchanges, bridges and wrapped or staked products of another protocol's asset are not ranked: transit TVL and receipt TVL are not a protocol waiting on a launch of its own.
